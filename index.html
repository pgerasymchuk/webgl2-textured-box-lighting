Click on the canvas to change the texture. Press these keys to manipulate cube:

  • ↑,↓ - Open/Close lid
  • ←,→ - Rotate around Y-axis
  • 2,8 - Rotate around X-axis
  • 4,6 - Rotate around Z-axis
  • 0,1 - Move along Z-axis
  • A,D - Move light source along X-axis
  • W,X - Move light source along Y-axis
  • 3,5 - Move light source along Z-axis
